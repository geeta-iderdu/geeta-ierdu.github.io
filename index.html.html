About

A little about my journey

I am an Educational Psychology and Guidance student with a background in Special Education, and I am interested in understanding how students learn, develop, and experience education. My academic journey has gradually led me from working with learners with diverse educational needs to exploring broader questions around student well-being, motivation, psychological assessment, and inclusive education.

I enjoy connecting psychological theories with practical educational settings. Through teaching, research projects, training module development, and my experience in inclusive educational environments, I have had opportunities to work with both learners and educators and to see how much the right support can influence a learner's experience.

My current research interests include student well-being, educational psychology, psychological testing and measurement, inclusive education, and child and adolescent development. I am particularly interested in research that is relevant to the Bangladeshi educational context.

I am continuing to develop my skills in research, academic writing, data analysis, and educational practice, with a long-term interest in building a career in research and academia.
